YardPour: Concrete Math Made Simple

Before you order bags or a ready-mix truck, measure length, width and depth in feet, multiply them for cubic feet, then divide by 27 for cubic yards. Add about 10% for spillage and uneven ground.

Example: a 10 x 10 ft slab at 4 inches thick is 33.3 cubic feet, about 1.23 cubic yards, so order roughly 1.35 yards.
